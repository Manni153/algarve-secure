What our Home Networking service covers

Every camera, alarm and access control device in a property depends on a network connection that doesn’t drop out. A poorly planned network is one of the most common reasons security systems become unreliable.

  • Structured cabling for a stable, wired backbone
  • Organised network racks, so equipment is installed properly rather than left as a tangle of cables
  • Backup power (UPS) to keep the network running through a power interruption
  • Telecommunications infrastructure connecting communication and access systems
  • A network built to support CCTV, alarms and access control running reliably
  • Suited to homes, offices and larger properties with more equipment to connect

Why It Matters

In Detail

Home Networking, done properly

Structured Cabling

A wired, structured cabling backbone gives cameras, alarms and access control a stable connection to rely on, rather than depending on Wi-Fi alone.

Network Rack & Infrastructure

Equipment is installed into an organised, labelled rack rather than left as a loose pile of boxes and cables — easier to maintain and to troubleshoot if something needs attention.

Backup Power (UPS)

A UPS keeps the network — and whatever depends on it, like cameras and alarms — running through a short power interruption instead of dropping offline.

Built to Support Your Other Systems

Networking is the layer everything else sits on top of. It’s set up with your cameras, alarms and access control in mind, not as a separate, disconnected job.
